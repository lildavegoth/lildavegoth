my friend and i went to the beach. the sand was warm and soft. we built a big sand castle. we used a red bucket and a blue spade. the waves came close but not too close. we saw small crabs in the wet sand. they moved side to side very fast. we found pretty shells of many colors. some were white, some were pink. we put them on top of our castle. the sun made the water shine like gold. we ate ice cream that was cold and sweet. then we swam in the cool blue sea. the water felt nice on our skin. we laughed and splashed for a long time. it was the best day at the beach.
