the cat sat on the blue mat. it watched a small bug fly. the bug went up and down. the cat tried to catch it. but the bug was too quick. a girl came into the room. she had a bowl of fish. the cat meowed for some food. the girl gave it one fish. the cat ate it all up. then it drank some water. now the cat felt sleepy. it curled into a soft ball. the girl petted its soft fur. soon the cat was dreaming. it dreamed of mice and birds. what a good day it had.
