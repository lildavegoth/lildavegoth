in my small garden I plant flowers. i have red roses and yellow daisies. the sun helps them grow tall and strong. i water them each morning with my green can. bees come to visit the bright blooms. they buzz from flower to flower. i watch them work and never get stung. butterflies with blue wings come too. they land on the leaves to rest. birds sing songs from the old tree. sometimes a fat worm comes up from the dirt. a black bird sees it and flies down fast. the garden is full of life and joy. i sit on a bench and smile. my garden is my happy place.
