Playing any videos in background on YouTube app is **impossible** except you pay or use **YouTube Revanced**, but in this Article i'll guide you to use it for free without third party app and no payments needed.

# Notes →
In this guides, i'll ask you to use **Cromite** as your main browser for now on because it's needed for this to happen. Also this method doesn't needs rooted devices or even UBL (Unlock Bootloader)

# Guides →
1. Download **Cromite** app
2. Follow this **Article** guides: **[Chrome Extensions in Android](https://kakoi-kiraku-home.vercel.app/pages/articles/article-page.html?slug=chrome-extension-in-android)**
3. Install this **Extension**: **[ViolentMonkey](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/violentmonkey/jinjaccalgkegednnccohejagnlnfdag)**
	- **Ignore the warning that this Extension is not available and install it**.
4. After installations, click 3 dots on the right corner of Cromite app and open Extensions
	- You'll see **Violentmonkey** there (This mean ViolentMonkey is installed and running)
5. Go back to Home of Cromite app, and click extensions menu on the right side of **Search Bar**, click **Violentmonkey** and then click **Settings**
	- You'll be in the ViolentMonkey app
6. Install **YouTube Background Playback** User Script from **[UserScripts Gallery](https://kakoi-kiraku-home.vercel.app/pages/userscripts-gallery.html)**
7. After the User Script is installed go to [YouTube](https://m.youtube.com/) website app, and try if it's working
