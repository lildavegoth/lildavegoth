For some users who doesn't and not using a Keyboard with auto save learned word, or you're the one who have a custom ROM on your device? you must be always loses learned words from your keyboard, don't you worry about it any more, **User Dictionary Manager** is here for you!

**User Dictionary Manager** aka **UDM** was made for the most common problems like i say above, including me are facing the same issue about it, because i use custom ROM and use third party Keyboard app.

# How it Works→
- **UDM** must have a permissions as a **Virtual Keyboard** or **On Screen Keyboard**. (simply just make **UDM** app as an **On Screen Keyboard** and you'll ready to use the app.)
- When the app has permission as a keyboard app, it will read all your **Dictionary** from the system and you can manage them whatever you wish, like edit, delete even add new words.
- As i say, this app will read all your learned words of **Android System Dictionary** or a Keyboard, but don't you worry about it, **UDM** can also be used in offline or just disable internet access for **UDM** if you don't trust the app.

# Guides →
> **Installations**

1. Download **UDM** app from **Play Store** or **Aurora Store** — [User Dictionary Manager](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.usr.dict.mgr)
2. After the app installed, you'll see new **UDM** app in your app drawer, open it and give permission as an **On Screen Keyboard**
3. Go back to the app and then click **Edit** button to manage all of your learned words

> **Advanced**

- As i say before, use **Edit** to manage your learned words
- Use **Clean** to removes all duplicates words
- Use **Delete** to literally removes everything of your leaned words (**This cannot be undone so be careful**)
- Use **Export** to save or backup your learned words inside a single .txt file. You need to choose the options before exporting, here's the details:
	- **File Path**: You must give permission to folder acces for the app, so **UDM** can generate .txt file. Choose where the exported file will be backed up
	- **File Formats**: **UDM** & **Gboard**. This meant for the words format inside .txt file only (Recommended: **UDM** format for better and universal usage for all keyboards)
	- **Export Language**: **Specific** language or **Neutral Language aka All**. You're free to choose what language you want to export (Recommended: **All** to export the entire learned words in a single .txt file)
- Use **Import** to restore all learned words into the system. **Import** menu has exact the same options as **Export** menu, and you need to remember what format is your current .txt file is. Same options but with some notes:
	- Choose **External** for the file path, and don't choose **Internal**!

# Notes →
**UDM** is great right? I've been use **UDM** since January 2025 and even still using it right now in Android 16 device.
**UDM** is useful indeed, but it can also be annoying sometimes..
If **UDM** still activated as **On Screen Keyboard** and you accidentally click switch keyboard, it will automatically switched the keyboard to **UDM**, but **UDM** is not a real keyboard app at all, so you'll not be able to typing anything and you MUST go to the system settings and switch back the keyboard to your own real keyboard app. To prevent this problem, use **UDM** only when you needed and always to disable **UDM** as **On Screen Keyboard**.
