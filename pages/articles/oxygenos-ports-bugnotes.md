# [OxygenOS Rev2](https://t.me/hiralayastuff/1123) →
Android 15 Port by Mahirooo | Revived Kernel
- device lags after reboot, so wait for a second until it's stable then use your device as usual
- doesn't support Dolby or any equalizer mods for Games (only supports for Music & Videos apps, AML & ACP can't fix this)
- can't access USB Configuration if USB Debugging is not enabled. it means you can't use MTP to access device storage via PC (i guess it's not bug, i put it in here just for reminder)
- screen dimmed on media picker
- when long press on a link in any browser apps, it will immediately open the link in new tab (already tested with different rom and it's only happened in this rom) - Video Included
- [known bugs](https://t.me/hirateam_info/8):
  - Face Unlock (Can't Fix)
  - Oplus Privacy (Can't Fix)
  - 64MP, HDR Camera
