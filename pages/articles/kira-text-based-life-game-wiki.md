# Adventure System →

**The World**

- A new random **map** is selected every day:  
  `Dark Forest`, `Crystal Caverns`, `Sunken Ruins`, `Haunted Battlefield`, `Dragon's Peak`.
- The Adventure view shows today's map and a random enemy.

**Player Stats**

| Stat | Default |
|------|---------|
| Health | 5,000 |
| Mana | 500 |

Equipment is set in the **Gear** menu (Weapon, Armor, Off‑hand, Buff, Pet) and uses items from your inventory.  
You can adventure without gear, but **without** any gear, a "without a Gear!" warning is shown.

**Pets**

Pets are equipped in the **Gear** menu under the **Pet** slot.  
If a pet is equipped, a special button appears in battle **after the Skill button**.

| Pet | Effect | Cooldown |
|-----|--------|----------|
| 🦄 Unicorn | Heals the player for **20% of max health** (1,000 HP) | 3 turns |
| 👾 Armed Minions | Deals **570 damage** to the enemy | 5 turns |
| 🐍 Deep Water Snake | **Passive** – 2% chance to instantly drown the enemy (any tier) on each attack or skill | None (passive, no button) |
| 🐋 Blue Whale | **Active** – fully restores player Health and Mana (5,000 HP / 500 Mana) | 15 turns |
| 🐉 Sea Dragon | **Passive** – triples all damage dealt by the player (Attack and Skill) | None (passive, no button) |

- **Passive pets** (Deep Water Snake, Sea Dragon) have **no button** in battle — their effect is automatic.  
- **Active pets** (Unicorn, Armed Minions, Blue Whale) require clicking their button to trigger the ability.

---

**Battle Mechanics**

1. **Attack** – 15‑30 base damage (+8‑15 if Iron Sword equipped).  
2. **Skill** – costs 30 Mana, deals 30‑55 base (+12‑20 if weapon).  
3. **Pet Action** (if pet equipped) – appears after Skill, on its own cooldown:  
   - 🦄 Heal: restores 1,000 HP (3‑step cooldown).  
   - 👾 Pet Attack: deals 570 damage (5‑step cooldown).  
   - 🐋 Blue Whale: fully restores HP & Mana (15‑step cooldown).  
4. **Flee** – chance of success decreases with higher enemy tier:  
   `flee_chance = max(0.2, 1.0 - (tier × 0.15))`.  
   If flee fails, the enemy attacks before you continue.

After your action, the enemy attacks (10‑25 damage, reduced by shield/armor).

**Buff Effects (from the Buff slot)**

| Buff | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Rainbow Tears | Increases max HP to **7,500** (cannot exceed). Heals **5% of max HP** every 15 turns. |
| Blue Pearl | Restores **5% of max Mana** every 15 turns (caps at 500). Reduces incoming enemy damage by **20%**. |
| King's Ring | Makes the **Village Inn** free (rest costs 0 coins). |

---

**After Victory – The Inn**

After winning a fight, there is a **25% chance** to find a **Village Inn**.
- The Innkeeper asks for **1,000 coins** to let you rest and restore:
  - **10% of max HP** (500 HP)
  - **5% of max Mana** (25 MP)
- If you own the **King's Ring** (equipped in the Buff slot), resting in **Inn** is **free**.
- You can choose to pay (or rest for free) or decline. Declining simply continues the adventure.
- After resting, a **Continue** button appears to proceed.

---

**Losing a Battle**

If you lose a fight (or fail to flee):
- You may **drop items from your inventory** (droppable items: cooked meals, forged weapons/armor). Up to 3 of a random droppable item can be lost.
- Additionally, your equipped **Weapon** and **Armor** each have a **30% chance** to be **unequipped and lost**.
- If nothing is lost, the message shows “Nothing”.

After defeating **10 enemies** in a single exploration, you instantly receive:
- **10,000 coins**
- **50 King's Emblems**

The counter resets, and you continue facing the next enemy.

# Level & Shop →
(This feature is still under maintenance)

You can check your own Level by using **/level** command, and this menu will appear:

```
# Level [current level] →
**[current xp]xp — [target xp]xp**

# Ranks →
* Username (Level)
...
```

- Ranks are paginated (5 per page) and ordered by level descending, then XP.

**XP Gain**

| Condition | XP Earned |
|-----------|-----------|
| Normal message (≤ 50 characters) | 11 XP |
| Long message (> 50 characters) | 15 XP |
| Level up target | 250 XP × current level |

- XP is gained per chat message (bots ignored).
- When leveling up, the target xp increases proportionally.

**Level Shop**

The **Level Shop** is accessible from the `/level` menu. Items change **daily at 06:00 (GMT+7)**.  
Each item can only be purchased **once per account**.

| Item                   | Price (XP) | Effect                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🐋 Blue Whale          | 2,600      | Adds 🐋 Blue Whale to Life inventory (Adventure pet)                                                               |
| 🐉 Sea Dragon          | 7,000      | Adds 🐉 Sea Dragon to Life inventory (Adventure pet)                                                               |
| Green Thumb            | 1,500      | Permanent upgrade – crops grow 20% faster                                                                          |
| Lucky Pickaxe          | 1,500      | Permanent upgrade – 15% chance for double ores when mining                                                         |
| Bonus Emblems          | 12,000     | +30 King's Emblems                                                                                                 |
| Unlimited Watering Can | 9,000      | Adds 🪣 Unlimited Watering Can (never consumed)                                                                    |
| 5000 Coins             | 2,500      | +5,000 coins to Life wallet                                                                                        |
| Special Role           | 5,000      | Grants the **Special** role in the server (mentions @everyone, bypass slowmode, create polls, change own nickname) |

- **Green Thumb** and **Lucky Pickaxe** are permanent life upgrades – their effects automatically apply.
- **Unlimited Watering Can** appears in your inventory; using it on the farm does not consume it.
- XP is **deducted** immediately upon purchase. Levels may drop if the remaining XP falls below the threshold.
