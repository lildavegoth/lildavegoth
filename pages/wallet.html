Privacy & Data Security

Local-Only Storage: All your financial data (cards, transactions, balances) is stored exclusively in your Browser's Local Storage. Nothing is sent to any server or external database. No Data Collection.

Complete Privacy: Your financial information never leaves your device. Not even the developer (lildavegoth) can access your data.

Browser-Based: Data persists only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data will also delete this app data. Use the Backup Storage feature in this Kakoi Kiraku Homepage to backup your data.

No Tracking: This application uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. Your usage is completely private.
