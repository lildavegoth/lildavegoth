About User Dictionary Manager

This tool allows you to create and manage a custom dictionary for keyboard autocorrect and shortcuts. It's perfect for:

  • Personal Shorthand: Create shortcuts for frequently typed phrases or complex words
  • Special Characters: Easy access to emojis, symbols, or special formatting
  • Workflow Optimization: Speed up your typing with custom autocorrect entries
  • Creative Writing: Store character names, world-building terms, or unique spellings

Privacy Note: All data is stored locally in your browser. No information is sent to any server - your dictionary remains completely private.
