How It Works

  • Add Links: Save any URL with a title, description, and image URL
  • Open Links: Click anywhere on a card to open the link in a new tab
  • Local Storage: All data is saved in your browser only
  • Edit & Delete: Use the buttons to manage links (buttons won't open the link)
  • Import/Export: Backup your links as HTML (Netscape format) or JSON (with Base64 images)
  • No Account Needed: Everything works locally on your device
  • Search: Use the search box to find links by title, URL, or description
